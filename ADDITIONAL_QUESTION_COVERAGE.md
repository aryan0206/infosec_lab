## Verification standard

The generated program requires no source edit for the first, third, fourth and seventh rows' tested combinations. `test_general_builder.test_arbitrary_roles_permissions_and_pin` and `test_different_schema_aes_paillier_signature_aggregate` simulate the option 10 prompts by patching `input` and calling `master_builder.main()`; they do **not** launch `START_TOOLKIT.bat`. The saved BAT logs document two prior actual launcher runs; no new BAT generation was performed during this read-only audit. `test_general_builder.test_schnorr_generated_signature` calls `general_builder.generate` directly. A requested operation outside `general_builder.py:COMPONENTS` cannot be inferred or automatically assembled from a similarly named primitive.
